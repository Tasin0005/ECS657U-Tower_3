# Tower Defence (Prototype)

**Play in your browser:** https://tasin0005.github.io/ECS657U-Tower_3/build/index.html

## How to play

The dark evil wizard is attacking the fortress. Defend it by placing wizards along the two enemy paths.

1. **Choose a wizard** in the shop on the right: Basic Wizard (2 points) or Advanced Wizard (5 points, unlocked with Magic Training).
2. **Click an empty cyan square** to place it. Each square holds one wizard.
3. **Press Start Wave.** Enemies come down both paths towards the fortress.
4. **Wizards attack automatically** when enemies are in range.
5. **Kills earn skill points.** Enemies that reach the fortress take points away.
6. **Spend points in the Skill Tree** in order: Physical Magic → Magic Training → Ultimate.
7. When a wave is cleared, press **Start Wave** again for the next one.

**Win:** unlock the **Ultimate** skill to defeat the dark wizard.

**Lose:** if a breach takes your points below zero, your most recently unlocked skill is locked again and your points reset to 0. If you're left with only Basic Magic, the fortress falls.

**Controls:** mouse only. Click **Reset** (bottom-left) at any time to start over, or **Restart** on the victory or defeat screen to play again.
